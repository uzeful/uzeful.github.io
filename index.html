Yu Zhang

Previously in 2020 and 2015, I respectively obtained Ph.D. degree in Department of Electronic Engineering at Tsinghua University, and M.Eng. degree in School of Astronautics at Beihang University.

Now I'm an Associate Professor with the Department of Aerospace Intelligent Science and Technology, School of Astronautics, Beihang University.

My research interests include computer vision, (medical) image processing and machine learning.
